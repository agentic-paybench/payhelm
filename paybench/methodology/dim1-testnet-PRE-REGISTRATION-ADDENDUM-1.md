## Correction of fact

Sections 6.5 and 11 and the host pin describe the T1 vantage (label `T1-devbox`) as "England, United Kingdom" and both vantages as "in Europe". The T1 host's network path originates in South Africa, as its public address registration and the content-delivery edges its reads reached show, in the confirmatory blocks and in the pre-freeze dry runs of 2026-10-14. The description was wrong when the document was frozen; no host, configuration or procedure changed between the freeze and the run.

The two vantages remain network-distinct and geographically distinct, which is what section 6.5 requires. The words "though both are in Europe" and the sentence "no claim is made about a client measuring from another continent" in section 11 are withdrawn: one vantage measured from Africa and one from Europe.

The measurement protocol, the analysis plan and every anchored file are unchanged by this addendum. The hardware pin held for both T1 blocks; the memory disclosure in the run record applies.
